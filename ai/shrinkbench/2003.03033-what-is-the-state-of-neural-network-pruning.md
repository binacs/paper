# What is the State of Neural Network Pruning?

## TL;DR

This paper presents a meta-analysis of neural network pruning research, revealing a lack of standardized benchmarks and metrics that hinders fair comparison. It introduces ShrinkBench, an open-source framework for standardized evaluation, and demonstrates its use to compare pruning techniques and avoid common pitfalls.

## Problem & Motivation

Neural network pruning has been extensively studied, but the field lacks standardized benchmarks and evaluation metrics. This makes it difficult to compare different pruning techniques or assess progress over time. The absence of consistent evaluation protocols leads to incomparable results and potential pitfalls in method comparison.

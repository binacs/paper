# What is the State of Neural Network Pruning?

## TL;DR

A meta-analysis of neural network pruning literature revealing a lack of standardized benchmarks and metrics, and the introduction of ShrinkBench, an open-source framework for standardized evaluation of pruning methods.

## Problem & Motivation

Neural network pruning research lacks standardized benchmarks and metrics, making it difficult to compare pruning techniques or measure progress over the past three decades. This deficiency is substantial enough to hinder meaningful comparisons and assessments of advancements.

## Key Ideas

- Meta-analysis of 81 papers and hundreds of pruned models reveals inconsistent evaluation practices.
- The community lacks standardized benchmarks and metrics, impeding fair comparisons.
- Concrete remedies are proposed to address these issues.
- ShrinkBench is introduced as an open-source framework to facilitate standardized evaluations.
- Comprehensive evaluation via ShrinkBench can prevent common pitfalls when comparing pruning methods.

## System Design

ShrinkBench is an open-source framework designed to standardize the evaluation of neural network pruning methods. It provides a consistent set of benchmarks and metrics, enabling fair comparisons across different pruning techniques. The framework is used to compare various pruning methods and demonstrate how comprehensive evaluation can avoid common pitfalls.

## Evaluation

The paper aggregates results across 81 papers and prunes hundreds of models under controlled conditions. It uses ShrinkBench to compare various pruning techniques, showing that its comprehensive evaluation can prevent common pitfalls when comparing pruning methods. ## Takeaways

- Standardized benchmarks and metrics are crucial for meaningful progress in neural network pruning.
- The lack of standardization has made it hard to compare techniques or assess progress over decades.
- ShrinkBench provides a practical solution for standardized evaluations.
- Comprehensive evaluation can prevent common pitfalls in comparing pruning methods.
